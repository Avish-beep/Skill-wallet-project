1. The Triad of Psychological Burden

Analysis of the 101 undergraduate respondents reveals an extraordinarily high co-occurrence of symptoms:

  • Depression: {{ stats.depression_count }} students ({{ stats.depression_pct }}%) suffer from clinical depressive symptoms.
  • Anxiety: {{ stats.anxiety_count }} students ({{ stats.anxiety_pct }}%) experience recurrent generalized anxiety.

2. The 90.5% Campus Treatment Void

The starkest disparity surfaced by this dataset is the gap between clinical need and healthcare utilization:

  • 63 out of 101 students report at least one diagnosable mental health condition.
  • Yet, only 6 of those 63 students sought professional counseling or medical consultation.
  • {{ stats.treatment_gap_pct }}% of suffering students navigate their symptoms without institutional or professional assistance.
