Project Problem Statement

Student mental health in higher education institutions has emerged as a silent epidemic. Accelerated academic demands, transitional life stress, and social pressures frequently trigger depressive symptoms, anxiety disorders, and acute panic episodes.

Despite rising distress levels, stigmatization and institutional opacity obscure the true magnitude of student suffering. This project bridges this information gap through rigorous exploratory data analysis, visual storytelling in Tableau, and an integrated web dissemination portal.

Key Analytical Objectives

  • Quantify baseline prevalence of depression, anxiety, and panic attacks across undergraduate cohorts.
  • Stratify respondents into clinically grounded risk categories (Low, Moderate, High Risk).
  • Evaluate academic standing (CGPA) and seniority (Year of Study) as potential correlates of psychological vulnerability.
  • Quantify the campus treatment gap to provide empirical justification for institutional counseling expansion.
