# Tableau Storyboard Architecture
## Story Title: The Unseen Struggle: Decoding Student Mental Health Ecosystems

A structured 6-scene analytical narrative designed for academic evaluation, peer review, and institutional decision-makers.

---

### Scene 1: The Landscape of Student Mental Health
- **Storyboard Tab Title:** 1. Ecosystem Overview
- **Worksheet / Visual Assets Used:** Donut Chart of Risk Tiers (`Worksheet 5`) + Executive KPI Banner (`Worksheets 1, 2`)
- **Analytical Purpose:** Establish the scope, sample demographics, and overall baseline distribution of mental health across the student population.
- **Key Insight:** **62.4% of surveyed students** report experiencing at least one major clinical psychological condition (Depression, Anxiety, or Panic Attacks), with **35.6% categorized as High Risk**.
- **Narrative Explanation:** Contrary to common assumptions of undergraduate life being largely carefree, the survey reveals that over six in ten students actively navigate mental health challenges. This baseline indicates that distress is widespread rather than an isolated phenomenon.

---

### Scene 2: The Triad of Psychological Distress (Depression, Anxiety & Panic)
- **Storyboard Tab Title:** 2. Clinical Conditions
- **Worksheet / Visual Assets Used:** Condition Prevalence Bar Chart (`Worksheet 4`) + Comorbidity Frequency Histogram (`Worksheet 10`)
- **Analytical Purpose:** Dissect the specific prevalence of individual conditions and investigate the degree of comorbidity among respondents.
- **Key Insight:** Depression (34.7%), Anxiety (33.7%), and Panic Attacks (32.7%) affect nearly identical proportions of students. Furthermore, **15.8% (16 students) experience all three conditions simultaneously**.
- **Narrative Explanation:** Mental health symptoms rarely exist in silos. The high correlation between panic attacks and severe anxiety indicates that physiological responses frequently accompany generalized academic worries, culminating in debilitating comorbid episodes.

---

### Scene 3: Demographics & Social Variables
- **Storyboard Tab Title:** 3. Demographic Profiles
- **Worksheet / Visual Assets Used:** Gender-Based 100% Stacked Bar Chart (`Worksheet 6`) + Age Group Onset Trend (`Worksheet 12`)
- **Analytical Purpose:** Investigate whether demographic variables (gender, age cohorts, marital status) moderate the severity of psychological symptoms.
- **Key Insight:** Female students represent 74.3% of the survey cohort and carry higher absolute counts of anxiety and depression; symptom prevalence peaks significantly during early undergraduate years (ages 18–20).
- **Narrative Explanation:** Underclassmen transitioning from secondary schooling into university environments demonstrate the sharpest vulnerability curve, underscoring the critical need for peer mentorship and orientation-phase psychological screening.

---

### Scene 4: Academic Rigor vs. Mental Health
- **Storyboard Tab Title:** 4. Academic Pressure & CGPA
- **Worksheet / Visual Assets Used:** CGPA Tiers vs Risk Profile (`Worksheet 8`) + Faculty Heatmap (`Worksheet 9`)
- **Analytical Purpose:** Test the hypothesis that mental health distress is correlated with academic performance tiers and specific disciplines.
- **Key Insight:** Mental health distress is **not confined to struggling students**. In fact, students in the top CGPA band (`3.50 - 4.00`) demonstrate high rates of anxiety and depression, suggesting perfectionism and performance anxiety are potent stressors.
- **Narrative Explanation:** High achievers often suffer in silence due to fear of stigma or loss of academic prestige. Concurrently, technical disciplines (Engineering and Computing) show localized distress clusters driven by rigorous coursework deadlines.

---

### Scene 5: The High-Risk Crisis & Campus Treatment Void
- **Storyboard Tab Title:** 5. The Treatment Gap
- **Worksheet / Visual Assets Used:** Campus Treatment Gap Waterfall / Diverging Bar (`Worksheet 11`) + Crisis KPI (`Worksheet 3`)
- **Analytical Purpose:** Expose the critical disparity between mental healthcare need and actual specialist consultation.
- **Key Insight:** **90.5% of symptomatic students (57 out of 63) have NEVER consulted a mental health specialist.** Only 6 students with identified conditions sought professional help.
- **Narrative Explanation:** This represents the most alarming finding of the investigation. Despite widespread suffering, systemic barriers—including institutional stigma, lack of accessible on-campus counseling, and low mental health literacy—deter over 90% of affected students from seeking professional care.

---

### Scene 6: Institutional Recommendations & Future Roadmap
- **Storyboard Tab Title:** 6. Strategic Interventions
- **Worksheet / Visual Assets Used:** Integrated Executive Dashboard (`Dashboard 1`) + Summary Policy Matrix
- **Analytical Purpose:** Synthesize empirical findings into actionable, high-impact recommendations for university administration and counseling centers.
- **Key Insights & Recommendations:**
  1. **Normalize De-Stigmatized Counseling:** Embed proactive mental wellness days and walk-in clinical triage during midterms and finals.
  2. **Faculty-Specific Support Channels:** Deploy dedicated counselors to high-stress departments (Engineering & IT).
  3. **Digital Screening & Peer Support:** Launch digital check-in tools and student-led mental health first aid networks to bridge the 90.5% treatment gap.
