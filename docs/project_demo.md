## 1. Verified Live Deliverables

### A. Tableau Public Interactive Dashboard
The final, interactive visual analytics dashboard is officially deployed and accessible on Tableau Public:
* **Live Dashboard Link:** [https://public.tableau.com/views/Student_Mental_Health_Analysis_sufiyan_17913953791380/AnalysingMentalHealthinStudentEcosystem?:language=en-US&publish=yes&:sid=&:redirect=auth&:display_count=n&:origin=viz_share_link](https://public.tableau.com/views/Student_Mental_Health_Analysis_sufiyan_17913953791380/AnalysingMentalHealthinStudentEcosystem?:language=en-US&publish=yes&:sid=&:redirect=auth&:display_count=n&:origin=viz_share_link)
* **Status:** Verified Live & Accessible (HTTP 200)
* **Dataset Scope:** 101 surveyed university students across 23 analytical dimensions and measures.
* **Core Views Included:**
  * Executive KPIs (Total Cohort: 101, High Risk: 28, Moderate Risk: 36, Untreated Symptomatic: 58)
  * Distribution of Mental Health Risk Levels (Low 36.6%, Moderate 35.6%, High 27.7%)
  * Self-Reported Conditions by Gender (Depression, Anxiety, Panic Attacks)
  * Treatment Gap Analysis (Highlighting that 90.6% of symptomatic students have not sought specialist care)
  * Academic Standing (CGPA Midpoint) across Risk Tiers
